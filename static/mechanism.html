The signals

What the fleet sees · hover to light it up

P

Parked now

Ambulances still waiting at a hospital gate, and for how long. A queue you can count from GPS.

W

Handover wait

How long the last few crews stayed before their patient was taken in. Recent waits count more (half-life 10 min).

T

Turn-aways

A crew arrives, then leaves with the patient still aboard, or taps "refused". The risk fades over about an hour.

?

No evidence

No crew has been there lately. The hospital is shown as unknown and assumed to be average, never free. The call decides.

score = drive + estimated wait + 40 × turn-away risk
All in minutes; lower is better. 40 min is the cost we charge for a likely refusal: the drive there, the wait at the gate, the drive on.
